# Product pitch — Stock Watchlist

Most watchlists show prices. Prices aren't change — they're numbers you
re-interpret each visit.

So an item is a **thesis**: entry zone, invalidation, target, catalyst.
"Meaningful" is judged against *that*, not a fixed 5%. A move is abnormal
relative to the stock's own volatility; a broken level is scored by what it
costs you.

Returning shows a diff, not a dashboard: what changed since **your** last visit,
literal was→now values, ranked by severity.

Two bets. Detection runs per-symbol, not per-user-per-symbol, so cost scales
with tickers, not users. And the engine is pure — no I/O, no clock — so it's
unit-tested and replayable.
